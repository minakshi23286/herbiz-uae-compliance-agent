# HerBiz: Compliance Agent — QA review: extraction correctness and Arabic/English handling

**Business line:** HomepreunerHub / HerBiz (Agent championship) · **Date:** 17 Oct 2026 · **Reviewer:** independent QA (Claude Code), read-only · **Worktree:** `CL_compliance_agent_app_ws_c` (ws-c-source-intelligence) · **Databases:** `data/CL_rag_c_d10_build.sqlite3` (main subject), `data/CL_c_readonly_copy.sqlite3` (reference) · **Not legal or tax advice.**

## Method (observed 17 Oct 2026)
- Sampled 27 in-scope articles: DL8 Art. 13–24 (12) and ER v3 Art. 1, 6–18, 14 (bis) (15). Read the stored passages against the pypdf and pdftotext page text.
- Compared the whole text: the full word sequence of every stored passage (in `seq` order) against an independent `pdftotext` reading-order dump, for DL8@v1 and ER@v3.
- Heading audit: classified every line that starts with "Article" in all 4 PDFs with `kb.ARTICLE_RX` / `footnote_on_page`. Also ran 23 crafted adversarial lines.
- Ran `python3 -m compliance_agent.rag_c extraction --db data/CL_rag_c_d10_build.sqlite3` (read-only; output in `scratchpad/qa/extraction_out.json`).
- Measured how much `extraction_check._unexplained` hides, using the real marker sets (DL8: 40 pages + 37 footnotes; ER v3: 66 pages + 52 footnotes).
- Tried the fallback extractor (`pdftotext -layout`) by monkeypatching `_pdf_pages` in memory. Nothing was written to disk.
- Scripts and dumps: `scratchpad/qa/scripts/gdiff.py`, `scratchpad/qa/gdiff_*.txt`, `scratchpad/qa/pages/`.

## What passed (evidence, observed)
- **No law text was dropped or added.** Apart from running headers, the global word diff (KB vs pdftotext) found only two kinds of difference: (a) a different reading order in the two-column definition tables (DL Art. 1, ER Art. 1); (b) pypdf word-split artefacts. Every number in the 27 sampled articles matches the PDF. Example: ER Art. 7 AED 375,000 / 30 days, Art. 8 AED 187,500, Art. 14(1) "20 (twenty) Business Days".
- **Article boundaries are right.** Line-start "Article" lines accepted as headings: DL 86, ER v1/v2/v3 77 each. That is exactly the set of real headings. All in-text references at line start were correctly rejected: 6 in DL, 16–19 per ER version (e.g. "Article 19 of this Decree-Law will exceed…"). Every passage begins with its own heading. ER Art. 1 (D10) and Art. 3 (bis) / 14 (bis) split correctly. No article numbers are missing.
- **Running-header removal is safe** on these files. No PDF page has a digits-only line, so the "#" rule removed no law text.

## Findings
| id | severity | finding | evidence | suggested fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| E1 | **High** | Footnote text sits inside the law text, often **in the wrong article** and in the middle of a sentence. Footnote markers are glued into titles. An answer about amendment history can wrongly say an article was amended, or miss that it was. | All 37/37 DL footnote lines and 52/52 ER v3 footnote lines are inside passages. Footnotes that sit in an article other than the one whose heading carries the marker: DL fn 8 (Art 5) in Art 6; fn 9 (Art 7) in Art 10; fn 10 (Art 13) in Art 14, splitting clause 4 ("…specified by the ⟨10 Article amended as per Federal Decree-Law No. 18 of 2022⟩ Executive Regulation…"); fn 11 (Art 15) in Art 17; ER fn 13 in Art 18. The footnote also splits ER Art 7(5) and DL Art 21(1)(a/b). Titles read "Mandatory Tax Registration10" and "Article 1 1". | Before splitting articles, cut the footnote block off the bottom of each page (lines matching the footnote pattern, from the first one down). Store footnotes as metadata on the article or clause whose text carries the marker. Strip trailing marker digits from headings. |
| E2 | **High** | `_unexplained` counts **every page number 1..N** as a "footnote marker", so it hides real legal-number differences. Its docstring ("20 vs 200 stays a mismatch") is true only for that one case. The check also drops 1-digit numbers and compares sets, not counts or positions. | With real markers (1–40 DL, 1–66 ER): if a ≥2-digit number disappears, the check ignores it for 155/267 (58%) of distinct numbers per article in DL and 248/385 (64%) in ER v3. Hidden pairs (tested): 30↔15 days, 30↔60, 12↔6 months, 20↔201, 187500↔87500, 375000↔1375000. Still caught: 20↔200, 375000↔37500, 2024↔2023, 100↔10. Of all single-digit edits (insert/delete/substitute) to legal numbers, 14% (DL) and 18% (ER) are fully hidden. Every deadline-sized number (≤66) is hidden. | Remove page numbers from `markers`: running headers are already stripped. Accept glue only for the marker of a footnote on the same page, and only at heading or line-end position. Compare numbers as an ordered multiset with context ("30 days", "AED 375,000", "Article 21"). Include 1-digit numbers. Add a regression test (30 vs 15 must fail). |
| E3 | Medium | The heading rule accepts some reference lines, and the check cannot see this. A bare line "Article 30" is accepted with no check. "Article 21 – 24 of the Decree-Law", "Article 21 - Clause 2 applies" and "Article 13 (2)" are also accepted. "Article 30 3" is accepted whenever any footnote 3 is on the page. The second extractor uses the same `ARTICLE_RX`, so the two extractors fail the same way. A false "Article N+1" just before the real one stays contiguous, so `structure()` misses it too. | Adversarial run: all 6 strings above match as HEADING. `footnote_on_page(page,'3')` returns True for an unrelated "3 Clause 2 amended…" footnote. None of these occurs in the current 4 PDFs (0 false headings), but a new layout or the fallback extractor could produce one. | Accept a heading only when its number is the expected next one (prev, prev+1, or prev with bis/ter); otherwise record it as a candidate. For the bare form, require a title-like next line. Drop `\(\d+\)` from group 2. Use a different heading rule in `extraction_check`, so the check is independent of the KB. |
| E4 | Medium | Some real headings would be missed, and three rule sets disagree with each other. These forms are not matched: "Article 3 bis" (no brackets), "Article 3 (ter)", "Article 13: Title", a 3-digit footnote marker ("Article 8 100"). `footnote_on_page` misses footnotes worded "This Article was amended…" or "Replaced by…", and footnotes that run onto the next page. In those cases the D10 heading is skipped and the article merges into the previous one: the original Article 1 bug comes back. | Regex tests: all of these are "-". `LOCATOR_RX` allows ter/quater, but `ARTICLE_RX` does not. `ARTICLE_RX` allows "(repeated)" and "(\d+)", but `LOCATOR_RX` does not. `extraction_check.marker_rx` allows This/The, but `kb.footnote_on_page` does not. | Use one shared heading grammar (bis/ter/quater/repeated, optional brackets, colon or dash) and one footnote grammar. Allow 1–3 digit markers. Also check the footnote on the next page. |
| E5 | Medium | The 3000-character "part" splits cut through words and sentences, with no overlap. Every part reports the whole article's page range. | All 30 split points (DL + ER v1–v3) fall mid-word. ER Art 14(9), the G1 article: "shall not absolve a Person fr ⟂ om having to comply…". DL Art 48 is cut mid-sentence. DL Art 45 part 2 is 16 characters ("Two – Exemptions"), which is why the DL8 `structure` check fails (near_empty). | Split at clause boundaries (`^\d+\.`, `^[a-z]\.`, `^\d\)`), with a small overlap. Repeat the article heading at the top of each part. Give each part its own page range. |
| E6 | Medium | Title and Chapter headings are attached to the end of the previous article, so page ranges are too wide. | 27/86 DL articles and 17/77 ER v3 articles contain a Title/Chapter line. Only that line extends `page_end` in DL Art 10, 12, 33, 45, 64, 77 and ER Art 18, 45, 72. Example: ER Art 18 is cited as "pp. 15-16" but sits wholly on p. 15; p. 16 holds only "Title Four – Rules Relating to Supplies". ER Art 1 ends with "Title Two – Supply". | Treat `TITLE_LINE` as a boundary. Keep the Title/Chapter as metadata on the following articles. Compute `page_end` from law text only. |
| E7 | Low–Med | pypdf breaks words apart. This hurts lexical search (FTS/BM25) and exact quote checks (`support_quote`). | KB vs pdftotext: 56 broken words in DL and 108 in ER v3, for example "Au thority", "Ta x", "opt ions", "fr om", "registrat ion", "Tax Procedu res". There are also spaced hyphens, "Decree -Law" and "12 -month". | Re-join the broken words using pdftotext tokens or a vocabulary built from the corpus. Normalise " -" to "-" before indexing. Keep the raw text for display. |
| E8 | Low | `rag_c extraction` fails on every version for known, explainable reasons, so a real failure would not stand out. | DL8: structure pass=False (Art 45 part 2). Second extractor pass=False on all 4 versions: ER Art 1 below the similarity threshold because of the table reading order; ER v2 Art 44/74 and v3 Art 74; DL Art 84. `articles_only_in_second: ['8337']` because `_second_units` lacks the `heading_no` repair. | Apply the same marker repair in `_second_units`. Keep an explicit, reviewed waiver list (article, reason, date) so the remaining failures signal real problems. |
| E9 | Low | The fallback extractor (`pdftotext -layout`, used when pypdf is absent) gives wrong status and leaves headers in. | Simulated: the trailing `\f` creates a phantom empty last page, so status is "partial" with "1 of 67 pages have no usable text layer". Running headers are **not** removed under -layout. Article counts still come out at 86/77. | Drop the final empty split after `\f`. Normalise internal whitespace in the `strip_running_headers` key. |
| A1 | Medium | The KB stores **English unofficial translations as `authority_type = primary`**, with `flags_json = []` and no language field. In the UAE the Arabic text prevails. The ER v3 running header says "As published by the Ministry of Finance", while page 1 says "This is not an official translation". | `source_versions`: all 4 PDF versions are primary with `[]` flags. The `passages` and `source_versions` tables have no language column. The Arabic check (addendum v1.1) was done by hand, outside the KB. | Add `language` and `text_status` ("unofficial_translation" or "official") to versions and passages. Every answer citing an EN passage should say "unofficial English translation; the Arabic text prevails". Any deadline, amount or threshold an answer depends on should be checked against the Arabic text, as was done for Art. 14(1). |
| A2 | Medium (future) | The Arabic PDF text layer is not reliable, and the pipeline is English-only in four places. `ARTICLE_RX` looks for the literal "Article". `words = [a-z]+` gives empty output for Arabic, so the similarity score means nothing. `NUM_RX` / `\d` match Arabic-Indic digits (٠–٩), but nothing maps them to ASCII. Nothing handles bidi order. | Addendum §3 (observed 16 Oct): the letter order is scrambled, e.g. "فر" for "في" and "الضريت ي" for "الضريبي". The rendered page had to be read visually. tesseract (OCR) is not installed. A number in parentheses inside RTL text is at risk of reversed digits or mirrored brackets (")02(" for "(20)"). | Arabic ingestion would need: (1) OCR of the rendered pages (tesseract + `ara`) or a verified text source, with a page-image check of every number; (2) NFKC normalisation of presentation forms, plus removal of tatweel and diacritics; (3) digits ٠–٩/۰–۹ mapped to 0–9, and a check that the number in words matches the digits ("(20) عشرين"); (4) an Arabic heading grammar: "المادة (14)", "مكرر" (bis), ordinal-word articles; (5) FTS5 `unicode61 remove_diacritics 2` or an Arabic analyser; (6) EN↔AR passages aligned by article key, with a numbers-agree check across languages; (7) Arabic recorded as the prevailing text. |
| A3 | Low | The Arabic evidence cannot be reproduced offline. The PDF was read from the WebFetch cache and was not stored in `data/raw` or in the lock file. The file is named "Law-No-8…" but holds the ER. | Addendum §2 records the sha256 `52d94bb0…`, 54 pages and the URL, but there is no snapshot in the project. | If the founder approves collecting it, register the snapshot through the normal collector, with its hash and language "ar", and record the file-name mismatch as a flag. |
